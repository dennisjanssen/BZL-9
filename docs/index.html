Install BZL-9

Flash the desk companion firmware to your ESP32-C6, straight from this page.

Your browser cannot talk to serial devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop — this does not work on iOS, and Android is unsupported. This page has to be served over HTTPS for the browser to allow serial access.

Build __VERSION__

What you need

  • A Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 (or a clone of that reference design).
  • A USB-C cable that carries data, not just power.
  • Chrome or Edge on a desktop.

No drivers. The ESP32-C6 has a built-in USB-Serial-JTAG peripheral rather than a separate bridge chip, so the board appears on its own once plugged in.

Then set it up

  1. When flashing finishes, the device restarts and shows its face.
  2. With no Wi-Fi credentials stored it starts its own access point, BZL-9-Companion — open, no password.
  3. Join it and open 192.168.4.1. Any unmatched URL redirects there, so most phones offer the page on their own. Enter your Wi-Fi details, your latitude and longitude, and your workday hours, then save.
  4. It reboots onto your network and is then reachable at bzl9.local, where everything else is configured.

What gets written

Two images: the firmware (bootloader, partition table and application) and a small filesystem holding the web dashboard. Both are needed — without the second the device boots but serves no pages.

The installer offers to erase the device first. Say yes on a new board. Erasing also clears saved Wi-Fi credentials and settings, so if you are re-flashing one you have already set up and want to keep those, decline it.

Later updates can go over the air from the dashboard instead, at /update.
